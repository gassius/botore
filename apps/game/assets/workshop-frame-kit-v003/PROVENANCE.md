# Provenance: kit v003.3
- **Author:** Game Art Director (Grok Bot) for Carlos González Rico, 2026-11-17–18 (Europe/Madrid).
- **Parts:** byte-identical to v003 / v003.1 / v003.2 (procedural SVG sources, CairoSVG raster). No part art changed.
- **Clips:** v003.2 authoring, re-baked in v003.3: knockout floor squash is held 3–4 frames then eases into the approved v003.1 defeat kneel (axe off-hand relocked on the way down, released after the floor); dagger attack has a coil→extend in-between and a smear covering coil-to-extension; axe recovery through follow-through is continuous and `poses.follow_through.axe` is visor-clear. Axe attack is capped at 56 frames (v003.2 was 51). Named angular-velocity exceptions — the coil→whip flip hidden under the whip smear, the pendulum→low and overshoot→idle basin changes, the back→rise in-between, and the first two axe hit_react frames — are listed in `validation/av-exceptions.json`. The validator also waives ≤45°/frame on any frame whose smear raster overlaps an arm. Rotations are baked: engines only play frames.
- **Static pose:** `poses.follow_through.axe` arms were re-solved so the weapon no longer enters the visor (0 px). Body and legs are unchanged. Victory, defeat and idle are unchanged from v003.1/v003.2.
- **Smears:** generated from the weapon tiles themselves (swept area of the striking end). Dagger thrust smears are swept from the coil pose to the current frame so the trail covers the full travel. Flat-banded in the kit's steel tones with the kit ink outline. No painted or generated imagery.
- **Previews:** rendered by the manifest-only assembler (`mfasm.py`) from `manifest.json` + `animation/clips.json` + the shipped PNGs. GIF/MP4 with Pillow and ffmpeg (libx264). Backgrounds: `environment-v001` (Repair Yard).
- **Inputs not used:** no generative-image models, no ElevenLabs, no network fetches, no third-party art.
- **Test paint:** `#6E8B3D` (Scrap Green) is a QA test paint only, not a catalog paint.
